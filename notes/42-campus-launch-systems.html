Field note 02 / Campus systems

A campus launch is a connected service

Candidates do not experience an org chart. They experience one journey across registration, communication, identity, access, learning systems, people, and the physical campus.

David Dacruz9 August 20266 minute read

On this page

One journeyConnected layersReadiness gatesAfter opening

Map the candidate’s journey end to end

The operational sequence begins before anyone reaches the building: discovery, registration, online selection, communication, identity checks, allocation, arrival, access, support, evaluation, selection, and onboarding.

Each transition needs an owner, a source of truth, and a recovery path. A correct database record is not enough if the candidate receives the wrong instruction or cannot enter the campus.

Treat the layers as one service

  • Identity: one person, consistent records, appropriate permissions.
  • Communication: dates, expectations, changes, and escalation routes.
  • Physical access: doors, opening hours, safety, and accessibility.
  • Technology: devices, network, accounts, monitoring, and support.
  • Learning operations: allocation, evaluation, staff coverage, and student welfare.
The integration question: what happens when a candidate is accepted in one system but missing, duplicated, or delayed in another?

Use readiness gates, not optimistic dates

A launch date should follow evidence. Run realistic rehearsals with candidate volumes, failed logins, access problems, last-minute changes, staff escalation, and communications under time pressure.

The campus is ready when the team can observe problems, make decisions, communicate clearly, and recover without depending on one person’s memory.

Opening is the beginning of the operating cycle

The first live cohort exposes assumptions that planning documents cannot. Keep time and capacity for observation, incident review, workflow correction, student support, and the next intake.

This note reflects the operating layer David worked across on the founding 42 Lisboa team. The documented scope is available in the 42 Lisboa campus launch case study.
