Working principles

How I examine technical work.

These are not universal laws. They are repeatable checks that make delivery risk easier to see.

Name the owner.

A workflow without an accountable operator is a demo. Someone needs authority to handle exceptions, change rules, and stop unsafe output.

Trace the whole journey.

Users experience one service even when the organisation splits it across forms, databases, permissions, inboxes, and teams.

Separate evidence from inference.

Logs, tests, source data, and direct observation should be distinguishable from a plausible explanation.

Design the recovery path.

Failure is operationally manageable when detection, escalation, rollback, and communication are part of the design.
